## 3.6 Overall Training Objective

The complete objective combines three terms: a segmentation loss that supervises region overlap, the boundary loss of Section 3.4, and the edge-weighted reconstruction loss of Section 3.5. The segmentation term and the reconstruction branch are inherited from the restored MAFF-Net, whereas the boundary loss and the edge weighting of the reconstruction constitute the proposed contribution.

Segmentation is supervised with a deeply supervised Dice loss. For a prediction $p$ and ground-truth mask $y$, the Dice loss is

$$\mathcal{L}_{\text{Dice}}(\sigma(p), y) = 1 - \frac{2 \sum \sigma(p)\,y + \varepsilon}{\sum \sigma(p) + \sum y + \varepsilon},$$

where $\sigma$ is the sigmoid and $\varepsilon$ a smoothing constant that keeps the ratio defined for empty masks. The restored decoder emits a prediction $p_i$ at each of its scales, and the segmentation loss aggregates the per-scale Dice terms with deep-supervision weights $w_i$,

$$\mathcal{L}_{\text{seg}} = \frac{1}{\sum_i w_i} \sum_i w_i\, \mathcal{L}_{\text{Dice}}(\sigma(p_i), y).$$

The remaining two terms were defined in full in the preceding subsections and are restated here only to fix notation. The boundary loss $\mathcal{L}_{\text{BDS}}$ compares the Sobel boundary of each smoothed prediction with that of the ground truth and aggregates the per-scale terms with the same weights $w_i$. The edge-weighted reconstruction loss $\mathcal{L}_{\text{EWR}}$ is the normalized boundary-weighted mean-squared error between the auxiliary reconstruction and the input, using the weight map $W(y) = 1 + \alpha B(y)$. Both terms are derived from the single boundary prior $B(y) = S(y)$, the Sobel response of the ground-truth mask.

The final training objective is the weighted sum

$$\mathcal{L} = \mathcal{L}_{\text{seg}} + \lambda_b(t)\,\mathcal{L}_{\text{BDS}} + \lambda_r\,\mathcal{L}_{\text{EWR}},$$

where $\lambda_b(t) = \lambda_b^{\max}\,\min(1, t/T_{\text{warm}})$ introduces the boundary term under the warmup schedule of Section 3.4 and $\lambda_r$ weights the reconstruction term. Training minimizes $\mathcal{L}$ over the trainable parameters of the framework, namely the perception adaptors, the fusion modules, the segmentation decoder, and the reconstruction decoder, while the SAM2 encoder remains frozen. Setting $\lambda_b = 0$ and $\alpha = 0$ reduces the objective to a deeply supervised Dice loss with a uniform reconstruction term, which is the supervision used by the restored MAFF-Net. The proposed objective therefore contains the restored baseline as a special case, and the two added terms can be enabled independently, which is the basis for the controlled ablation reported later.
