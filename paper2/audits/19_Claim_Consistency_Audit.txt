0. THE SINGLE MOST IMPORTANT FINDING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The paper's entire scientific framing is BOUNDARY FIDELITY. Section III-A, III-D
and III-H all rest on it: "must recover lesion boundaries faithfully",
"boundary-aware spectral representation", "Boundary-centric Dual Supervision".

NO BOUNDARY METRIC IS COMPUTED ANYWHERE IN THE REPOSITORY.

training/trainer.py::compute_metrics returns exactly six values — acc, dice, mIoU,
sensitivity, specificity, f1. There is no Hausdorff distance, no HD95, no
Boundary-IoU, no boundary-F1, no ASSD. Every quantitative result in the repository
is a REGION-OVERLAP measure. paper/tables/ablation_tables.md line 41 already
concedes this: "Add a boundary-specific metric (e.g., HD95 / Boundary-IoU) to
support boundary claims; not yet computed by the metric code."

Consequence: the paper proposes boundary-targeted machinery (Frequency Branch,
BDS, EWR) and then measures only region overlap. A Q1 reviewer will ask, in the
first paragraph of their report, why a boundary contribution is never evaluated
with a boundary metric. Region metrics are precisely the measure that Section III-H
argues is "comparatively insensitive to errors that are confined to the boundary" —
the manuscript itself explains why its own evidence is inadequate for its own claim.

This is fixable WITHOUT retraining: HD95 and Boundary-IoU can be computed from the
existing 21 canonical checkpoints in one evaluation pass. It is the highest-value
outstanding experiment in the project.

2. UNSUPPORTED CLAIMS (12)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Each must be removed, rewritten, or backed by a new experiment before submission.

N1  "The proposed method achieves the best Dice." [RISK: CRITICAL]
    EVIDENCE: paper2/results/final_benchmark_table.md. On the held-out test split
    MAFF-Net (Restored) beats MAFF-Net (Proposed) on ALL THREE datasets:
    0.9183>0.9169 (2016), 0.9116>0.9096 (2017), 0.8844>0.8701 (2018).
    The proposed model is not the best model in its own family.
    CORRECTION: do not claim best Dice. Either reposition around precision/
    specificity (where Proposed wins consistently), or run multi-seed experiments
    to establish whether the gap is real, or explicitly report Restored as the
    stronger variant and justify Proposed on other grounds.

N2  "State-of-the-art." [RISK: CRITICAL]
    EVIDENCE: no comparison against any published result exists. The repository
    implements four baselines (U-Net, Attention U-Net, UNet++, TransUNet), all
    trained locally. SGT-Net and Swin-UNet — named in the synopsis — are NOT
    implemented (Major finding M3, 11_Experimental_Pipeline_Audit). No external
    leaderboard or published number is cited anywhere.
    CORRECTION: delete every SOTA claim, or add a literature-comparison table
    clearly marked as figures quoted from the original papers, not reproduced.

N3  "Significantly improves / statistically superior." [RISK: CRITICAL]
    EVIDENCE: no random seed is set anywhere in the training path. A repository-
    wide grep for manual_seed/np.random.seed/random.seed/deterministic returns
    only models/maffnet_original_reconstructed.py:692 (a self-test block) and a
    comment in scripts/resplit_isic2017.py. Every number is a SINGLE RUN. No
    variance, no confidence interval, no significance test exists.
    CORRECTION: remove the word "significant" in its statistical sense entirely,
    or add >=3 seeds per configuration (requires adding seed support first).

N4  Any ablation-derived claim. [RISK: CRITICAL]
    EVIDENCE: paper/tables/ablation_tables.md is explicitly "STRUCTURE ONLY — no
    ablation experiments have been run"; every cell is a placeholder. Step 5 of
    13_Results_Completion_Plan was never executed. There is no experiment isolating
    the Frequency Branch, the Feature Alignment Module, or the Gated Fusion Module.
    CORRECTION: the three architectural contributions currently have ZERO
    experimental support for their individual value. Either run the ablation or
    drop all per-module efficacy claims. (The tables are honest placeholders — no
    values were invented. That is correct behaviour and must be preserved.)

N5  "Better preserves lesion margins on diffuse and irregular boundaries."
    (paper/figures/figure_captions.md, Fig. 7) [RISK: CRITICAL]
    EVIDENCE: no boundary metric exists (see section 0). The claim is also visual-
    only, and the referenced asset does not exist.
    CORRECTION: remove, or support with HD95/Boundary-IoU.

N6  BcDS efficacy. [RISK: HIGH]
    EVIDENCE: no A/B/C/D ablation (Table VI is empty). BDS and EWR are always on
    in every completed run. Their contribution is unmeasured, and the manuscript's
    claim that they "sharpen boundary delineation" (III-H) has no evidence.
    CORRECTION: III-H already describes BcDS as auxiliary — keep it descriptive,
    and add no efficacy claim without the ablation.

N7  Frequency-branch cutoff justification. [RISK: MEDIUM]
    EVIDENCE: Table V (cutoff sensitivity) is empty. cutoff=0.1 is an unjustified
    default; no experiment supports the choice.
    CORRECTION: state it as a fixed design choice, or run the sensitivity study.

N8  Reproducibility. [RISK: HIGH]
    EVIDENCE: no seed, no determinism flags, no per-run hardware record, and
    config.txt never records the --checkpoint argument for ANY run. train.py:188
    writes the "backbone: SAM2 Hiera-Large" line only when model=="maffnet", so the
    stabilized_maffnet runs have no backbone provenance at all. Two SAM2 weight
    files exist (checkpoints/sam2.1_hiera_large.pt and sam2_hiera_large.pt) and the
    repository does not record which was used.
    MITIGATION ALREADY ESTABLISHED: StabilizedMAFFNet raises ValueError when both
    encoder and checkpoint are None (stabilized_maffnet.py:53-54), so a real SAM2
    checkpoint was necessarily supplied — the proposed runs are NOT placeholder-
    encoder runs. Only the identity of the file is unrecorded.
    CORRECTION: do not claim reproducibility. Disclose single-run, unseeded status.

N9  paper/tables/performance_tables.md, Table II, U-Net Dice = 0.7613.
    [RISK: HIGH — unsourceable number]
    EVIDENCE: the canonical U-Net ISIC 2017 test Dice is 0.7229
    (experiments/unet_final_2017/metrics.json, produced in Step 3). No metrics.json
    anywhere in the repository contains 0.7613. That table predates the U-Net 2017
    test evaluation, which is precisely why Step 3 existed. The value cannot be
    traced to any artifact.
    CORRECTION: this table is SUPERSEDED. Replace with
    paper2/results/final_benchmark_table.md. Do not carry 0.7613 forward.

N10 paper/tables/performance_tables.md, Table III, Attention U-Net = "—".
    [RISK: MEDIUM]
    EVIDENCE: experiments/attention_unet_ISIC2018_final/metrics.json exists and is
    valid (test Dice 0.7662). The "incomplete" note is stale.
    CORRECTION: superseded by the new benchmark table.

N11 "The auxiliary reconstruction branch is omitted at inference." (III-A, III-B)
    [RISK: MEDIUM]
    EVIDENCE: StabilizedMAFFNet.forward always computes self.rdb(g4,g3,g2,g1) and
    always returns rec as the 5th element; there is no train/eval gate. The output
    is discarded by the evaluation code, not skipped by the model.
    CORRECTION: say the reconstruction output is used only during training and
    discarded at inference. Never claim an inference-time cost or latency saving
    from omitting it — that saving does not exist in this implementation.

N12 "The auxiliary reconstruction branch draws on the encoder-side features."
    (III-B) [RISK: MEDIUM]
    EVIDENCE: the call is self.rdb(g4, g3, g2, g1) — the FUSED representations, not
    encoder features. III-G states this correctly ("operates on the same fused
    multi-scale representations"), so the manuscript contradicts itself.
    CORRECTION: align III-B to III-G. Fused, not encoder-side.


3. CLAIMS NEEDING WEAKER WORDING (9)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
True as far as the evidence goes, but currently overstated or under-qualified.

W1  "Outperforms all baselines." SUPPORTED IN SUBSTANCE but needs qualification.
    The Proposed model does exceed all four baselines on Dice on all three
    datasets (2016: 0.9169 vs best baseline 0.8466; 2017: 0.9096 vs 0.8683;
    2018: 0.8701 vs 0.8259). Margins are large (+0.04 to +0.07) and unlikely to be
    noise. But it is a single unseeded run.
    => "consistently achieves higher Dice than all four baselines in a single-run
    evaluation under an identical protocol". Not "significantly".

W2  III-A "the frozen encoder, the convolutional decoder, and the auxiliary
    reconstruction branch are preserved without alteration" vs III-G "adapted here
    to consume the fused representations". The module CLASSES are imported
    unchanged (S17), so III-A is defensible at the class level, but the two
    sentences read as contradictory. => harmonise: components preserved, pathway
    adapted.

W3  Any margin below ~0.005 Dice. The Proposed-vs-Restored gaps on ISIC 2016
    (0.0014) and 2017 (0.0020) are far inside plausible single-run variation.
    => never characterise these as differences at all without seeds.

W4  Paper-faithful Reconstruction rows. Those three runs used the PLACEHOLDER
    encoder, not pretrained SAM2 (14_Checkpoint_Verification §3). => must be
    labelled at every appearance; never presented as a reproduction of the
    published MAFF-Net's reported performance.

W5  "mIoU". compute_metrics computes foreground IoU = TP/(TP+FP+FN), not a mean
    over classes. => report as IoU, or define explicitly on first use.

W6  Reporting Dice and F1 as separate columns. They are identical by construction
    (both 2TP/(2TP+FP+FN)); verified equal to 1e-12 in all 21 canonical files.
    => disclose the identity or drop F1.

W7  Precision. Not computed anywhere in the repository; the values in the
    benchmark table are algebraically derived and approximate under batch-wise
    averaging (18_Final_Benchmark_Table §F2). => keep the "derived" label or
    recompute properly.

W8  U-Net ISIC 2017 baseline. Trained 20 epochs; every other canonical run trained
    40. => disclose, or the baseline looks deliberately weakened.

W9  TransUNet ISIC 2018. Diverged after its best epoch (10); results come from the
    retained best checkpoint and are valid. => do not plot that training curve
    without explanation.

6. METRICS TO REMOVE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  F1            REMOVE. Identical to Dice by construction; presenting both implies
                two independent measurements and will be challenged.
  Precision     REMOVE, or recompute properly. Not measured anywhere; currently
                derived via P = 1/(2/Dice - 1/Recall), which is exact per batch but
                approximate on the batch-averaged values actually stored.
  "Recall" AND  REMOVE ONE. Sensitivity and Recall are the same quantity; listing
  "Sensitivity" both inflates the apparent breadth of the evaluation.
