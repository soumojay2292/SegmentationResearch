4. STOP CONDITION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The repository is considered RESULTS READY when ALL of the following hold:
  (1) Proposed MAFF-Net, Restored MAFF-Net, and Paper-faithful Reconstruction each
      have TEST-set metrics.json on ISIC 2016/2017/2018 (Step 2), so the proposed
      row is compared to baselines on the SAME split (resolves B1).
  (2) Every baseline has a valid TEST metrics.json on all three datasets — U-Net
      ISIC 2017 produced (Step 3) and TransUNet ISIC 2018 re-run (Step 4) — with
      no failed (Dice 0.0000) runs.
  (3) The performance-comparison table is regenerated solely from the canonical
      TEST metrics.json, with a single, documented provenance (Step 6) (resolves B4).
  (4) A component ablation on the primary benchmark (ISIC 2018) is available to
      populate the ablation table (Step 5); OR ablation is explicitly deferred and
      documented as future work.
  (5) Remaining gaps — multi-seed, cutoff/BcDS ablations, and figure assets — are
      documented as limitations/downstream work (B6, B7); they do not block a valid
      Experimental Setup + main Results write-up.
